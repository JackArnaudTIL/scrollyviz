Example prompts, token splits and probabilities are illustrative, written for teaching rather than taken from a real model.

Scaling curves: the Chinchilla fit (Hoffmann et al., 2022, arXiv:2203.15556) with constants refit by Epoch AI (Besiroglu et al., 2024, arXiv:2404.10102). Compute estimates: the standard rules of thumb of ≈ 6 × parameters × training tokens to train and ≈ 2 × parameters per token generated (Kaplan et al., 2020).
